Instructions

  1. Select your test duration before you begin typing.
  2. The timer starts automatically with your first keystroke.
  3. You may use the navigation arrows (<< / >>) to switch between exercises.
  4. Choose your backspace policy on the right (Full / One word / Deactivated).
  5. For Hindi exams, pick the correct keyboard layout (Inscript / Remington Gail / Remington CBI).
  6. Click Submit when you finish, or wait for the timer to expire.
  7. Words are evaluated against the source text. Speed is measured in WPM (5 keystrokes = 1 word).
